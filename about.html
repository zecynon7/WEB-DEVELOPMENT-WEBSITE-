Our story

How we started

Café BLK & BRWN started with passion in May 2021, opening its original branch in Barangay Capipisa, Tanza, Cavite. From that first location, the café grew steadily across Cavite and eventually into Metro Manila, including a branch at Festival Mall, Alabang.

What we serve

Our menu is built around espresso-based coffee, chai and chocolate drinks, iced drinks, Freskaró fruit juices, and food such as pasta and sandwiches — made for people who want to sit down, work, or catch up with a friend.

Mission

To serve well-made coffee, drinks, and food in warm, welcoming spaces where people can slow down, connect, and enjoy the moment.

Vision

To become a well-loved and recognizable café brand that customers in Cavite, Metro Manila, and nearby areas can easily discover, visit, and return to.
