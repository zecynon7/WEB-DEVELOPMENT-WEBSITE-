Who we are

Café BLK & BRWN started in Barangay Capipisa, Tanza, in May 2021. What began as one small coffee shop has grown into a multi-branch café and restaurant serving espresso-based coffee, chai and chocolate drinks, iced drinks, Freskaró fruit juices, and food like pasta and sandwiches.
